# Data sources and publication scope

This package publishes experiment summaries and fingerprints. It does not redistribute the underlying feeds, scraped pages, research excerpts, correspondence or vendor responses.

- **nflverse:** NFL player statistics, team statistics, schedules, weekly rosters, identifiers, snaps and play-by-play were used in scoring. See [nflverse-data](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data) and its source-specific notices. nflverse distributes data with CC BY 4.0 attribution requirements; upstream material can carry additional constraints. Miomaci normalized identities and calculated derived fantasy scores. The raw rows are omitted.
- **Tank01 / RapidAPI:** player and identity information was used during the football workflow. Raw responses and provider correspondence are omitted. This package makes no grant of upstream rights.
- **Exa and publishers:** web research was available during the draft. Research results and publisher text are omitted from this version. Source titles or text are not licensed by a fingerprint.
- **Official NFL team sites:** some scoring and participation decisions used official references. Where included in source receipts, the stable page URL and captured-byte hash identify the source; its HTML is withheld.
- **ESPN:** the experiment's scoring convention was frozen from its pre-draft decision record. This package does not claim parity with current ESPN settings or grant rights to ESPN material.
- **FantasyFootballBench:** Arya Tschand's project was studied as an architecture reference. This package includes no third-party implementation or private permission email and claims no affiliation or endorsement.

The download URL in a receipt is a retrieval location, not a guarantee that today's bytes match the archived hash or that redistribution is permitted. No article or source dataset is licensed merely by linking to it. Rights correspondence and private people mentioned in it remain outside this release.
